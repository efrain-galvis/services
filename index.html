How it goes

Small scope. Close loop. Something you can use.

No theater, no fake case studies. A straightforward way of working so you always know what we are doing and why.

  1. Tell me the problem

    A message on X is enough. What you are trying to ship, where it is stuck, and what good would look like.

  2. We agree on a useful slice

    A clear piece of work with a boundary: a prototype, a review, a feature, a set of evaluations. Not an open-ended retainer unless that is what you want.

  3. I work in the open with you

    You see the thinking as it happens — assumptions, dead ends, and the next decision. No disappearing for six weeks.

  4. You keep the work

    Code, notes, or a recommendation you can act on. The point is that your team can continue without me if that is the right call.
